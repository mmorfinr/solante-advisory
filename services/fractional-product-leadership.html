Fractional Product Leadership

Fractional Product Leadership for Startups and Growing Businesses

Senior product leadership without the full-time executive hire — hands-on strategic guidance across product direction, prioritization, roadmaps, KPIs, and execution.

Schedule a Strategy Call

Senior product leadership without the full-time executive hire.

Many companies reach a point where the product function needs more than advice — it needs ongoing decision-making, prioritization discipline, and executive presence. Solante embeds directly as that product leader, on a fractional basis, for as long as the business needs it. This is sometimes referred to as a Fractional CPO engagement, and the two terms are used interchangeably here.

When You Need Product Leadership — Not Just Advice

  • The founder is still carrying the product function — personally, alongside everything else
  • Product managers are in place — but there's no senior product leadership above them
  • The roadmap changes direction — every few weeks
  • Prioritization decisions are inconsistent — from one sprint to the next
  • Product priorities and business goals — have drifted apart
  • Leadership needs stronger visibility — into product metrics and performance
  • The company isn't ready for — a full-time CPO or VP of Product — or doesn't need one yet
  • The organization needs experienced leadership — through a transition or growth stage

How Solante Embeds

  • Weekly product strategy leadership
  • Roadmap management and ownership
  • Feature and opportunity prioritization
  • KPI review and product metrics
  • Product decision-making — not just recommendations from the sidelines
  • Executive guidance and cross-functional alignment
  • A consistent product operating cadence
  • Coaching for existing product managers

What Changes

  • Clearer, faster product decisions
  • Stronger, more consistent prioritization
  • A disciplined roadmap — instead of one that shifts every sprint
  • Better alignment — between product and business goals
  • A consistent operating rhythm — the team can rely on
  • Executive visibility — into product performance
  • Clearer accountability — around outcomes

Some engagements begin with a focused product strategy engagement before transitioning into ongoing fractional leadership. Both follow the Product Growth Framework™.

Who This Is For

  • Founder-led companies — where the founder is still running product personally
  • Startups entering a growth stage — that has outpaced current product leadership
  • Businesses — without senior product leadership in place
  • Teams — between product executives
  • Organizations — that need strategic product leadership without a full-time executive hire
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is fractional product leadership?

Fractional product leadership provides senior, hands-on product leadership — roadmap ownership, prioritization, KPI review, and executive guidance — on a part-time or embedded basis, without the cost and commitment of a full-time executive hire.

What is the difference between a Fractional CPO and a product consultant?

A consultant typically advises from the outside. A Fractional CPO embeds in the business, makes or guides real product decisions, and owns outcomes alongside the team on an ongoing basis.

When should a company hire a Fractional CPO?

Common signals include a founder still running product personally, inconsistent prioritization, a roadmap that shifts too often, or a growth stage that has outpaced the current product leadership structure.

How long does a fractional product leadership engagement typically last?

Most engagements run a minimum of three months, since meaningful product results require enough time to implement a roadmap and show measurable progress. Many continue longer as an ongoing arrangement.

Does Solante work directly with existing product teams?

Yes. Most engagements involve working directly with existing product managers and cross-functional teams, providing the senior leadership layer above them rather than replacing them.
