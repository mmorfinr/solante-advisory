Enterprise Transformation That Sticks

We bring rare depth to large-scale organizational change — having led it from the inside at some of the most complex technology organizations in the world. Operating models, program strategy, governance, and execution that sticks.

  • Operating Models — how the organization actually needs to work, not just an org chart redraw.
  • Program Strategy — sequencing transformation so it doesn't collapse under its own scope.
  • Governance — decision rights and accountability that hold up under pressure.
  • Cloud Transformation — migrating legacy infrastructure to cloud-native architecture without breaking the business while you do it.
  • Change Management — bringing people along, not just systems.

A representative engagement: leading the migration from legacy infrastructure to cloud-native architecture — improving scalability, resiliency, and service velocity at a scale most firms never attempt. The result was a 50x increase in deployment throughput (from 200 to 10,000+ monthly deployments) and over $2B in operational value delivered.
