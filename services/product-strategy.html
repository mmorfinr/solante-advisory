When the Product Direction Isn’t Clear

Product problems rarely announce themselves as strategy problems. They show up as symptoms — a roadmap that keeps shifting, a team executing hard without shared direction, or a growing list of things that could be built and no clear way to choose among them.

  • Too many competing priorities — and no clear way to rank them
  • A product vision built on instinct — rather than evidence
  • Roadmaps driven by the loudest request — rather than by strategy
  • A weak connection — between what customers need and what the business needs
  • Product ideas that haven't been validated — before resources go into building them
  • Difficulty deciding what not to build — as much as what to build
  • Teams executing without shared strategic alignment

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a product strategy consultant do?

A product strategy consultant helps translate customer insight and business goals into a clear, prioritized product direction — including what to build, for whom, and why — along with a roadmap the team can execute.

When should a startup bring in product strategy support?

Common points include before a major fundraising round, when priorities have become unclear, when a new product is being planned, or when the roadmap has become a list of requests rather than a strategy.

Can Solante help validate a product before we build it?

Yes. Product validation — testing assumptions against real customer and market evidence before committing resources — is part of the discovery work behind most engagements.

What's the difference between product strategy and a product roadmap?

Strategy is the reasoning: the customer, the opportunity, and the priorities. The roadmap is the sequenced plan that follows from that reasoning. A roadmap without strategy behind it tends to drift.

Can Solante help prioritize an existing roadmap?

Yes. Many engagements start with an existing roadmap that needs a clearer prioritization framework rather than a strategy built from scratch.
