AI Strategy

AI Strategy Consulting for Startups and Growing Businesses

AI strategy shouldn’t start with tools. It starts with business problems, customer needs, available data, and the outcomes that actually matter — then works backward to a prioritized roadmap.

Schedule a Strategy Call

Most organizations don't have an AI problem — they have a prioritization problem. There's no shortage of use cases, tools, or vendor pitches. What's missing is a clear line between an AI initiative and a business outcome worth pursuing.

Solante provides AI strategy advisory for founders, product leaders, and executive teams that need to move from scattered AI experiments to a prioritized strategy — grounded in actual business needs, available data, technical readiness, and the opportunities worth pursuing first.

From AI Interest to AI Strategy

Most AI conversations start in the wrong place — with a tool, a trend, or a competitor's announcement. AI strategy starts somewhere else: with the business problem, the customer need, and what's actually feasible given the data and systems already in place.

  • Too many possible AI use cases — and no consistent way to rank them against each other
  • Teams experimenting independently — without a coordinated strategy connecting the pieces
  • Pressure to adopt AI — without a clear business case behind the initiative
  • Uncertainty around build vs. buy — and which approach fits a given opportunity
  • Difficulty separating signal from noise — which opportunities carry real value versus which are interesting demos
  • AI initiatives disconnected from strategy — running apart from the broader product or business plan

How Solante Helps

  • AI Opportunity Assessment — where AI creates real leverage in the business, and where it's a distraction
  • Use-Case Identification & Prioritization — ranking opportunities by feasibility and business value, not novelty

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an AI strategy consultant do?

An AI strategy consultant helps identify where artificial intelligence can create real business value, prioritize among competing opportunities, and build a roadmap connecting AI investment to measurable outcomes — rather than starting with tools or trends.

How do you identify the right AI opportunities?

By starting with the business problem and customer need, not the technology. Opportunities are evaluated against feasibility, data readiness, and the value they'd create if executed well, then ranked accordingly.

Do we need an internal AI team before working with Solante?

No. Many clients engage Solante before they have any internal AI capability, specifically to determine what capability they need and in what sequence.

Can Solante help with build vs. buy decisions?

Yes. Build vs. buy evaluation is a core part of AI strategy work — matching the right approach to your resources, timeline, and the strategic importance of the capability.

How is AI strategy different from simply adopting AI tools?

Adopting tools without a strategy tends to produce disconnected pilots. AI strategy connects individual initiatives to a prioritized roadmap and measurable business outcomes, so the investment compounds rather than scatters.

Will the engagement include building or implementing the AI systems?

Solante's role is strategy, prioritization, and roadmap development, not software development. Recommendations are built to hand off cleanly to your internal team or technical partners.

What is AI strategy advisory?

AI strategy advisory helps business and product leaders determine where AI can create meaningful value, which opportunities deserve investment, and how those initiatives should fit into the broader product and business strategy. Solante focuses on the decisions that come before implementation: opportunity identification, prioritization, readiness, build-vs-buy decisions, roadmapping, and success metrics.
