Growth is more than acquisition.

It's common for a business to get better at bringing customers in the door while struggling with what happens after — activation, retention, and the monetization that actually sustains growth. Solante helps identify which of those levers matter most for your business right now.

Growth Is More Than Acquisition

  • Acquisition keeps increasing — but retention stays flat or worsens
  • Customers sign up — but never fully activate
  • Features ship — but adoption stays low
  • Revenue growth has stalled — even as usage grows
  • Pricing or monetization strategy — is unclear or untested
  • Teams track a dozen metrics — without knowing which ones actually matter
  • Growth initiatives run disconnected — from product strategy

How Solante Helps

  • Customer Journey Analysis — mapping where customers disengage and why

  • Monetization Strategy — pricing and packaging aligned with the value delivered
  • Growth Metrics & KPIs — the small number of metrics that actually indicate progress
  • Growth Opportunity Prioritization — where to invest first for the clearest return
  • Product-Led Growth Strategy — where the product itself is the primary growth engine

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is product growth strategy?

Product growth strategy identifies the specific levers — activation, retention, monetization, or adoption — that are constraining growth, and builds a prioritized plan to address the ones with the greatest impact.

How is growth strategy different from marketing?

Marketing typically focuses on acquisition: getting customers in the door. Growth strategy, as practiced at Solante, stays connected to the product — what happens after signup, why customers stay or leave, and how the product itself drives sustainable growth.

Can Solante help with retention and product adoption?

Yes. Retention and feature adoption are frequently the highest-leverage areas, addressed through customer journey analysis and product strategy work.

How do you identify the highest-value growth opportunities?

Through a growth diagnostic that examines the full customer journey and business model, then prioritizes opportunities by potential impact and feasibility rather than by which is easiest to measure.

Do we need a dedicated growth team?

No. Many clients engage Solante specifically because they don't yet have — or don't need — a dedicated internal growth function.
