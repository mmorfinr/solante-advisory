Our Methodology

The Product Growth Framework™

Our eight-phase Product Growth Framework™ brings structure to product strategy, planning, and execution. It's not a template — it's a systematic process that takes you from business understanding to validated strategy to executable roadmap, with clear deliverables at every phase.

8 Phases · Structured Deliverables

From Business Understanding to Executable Roadmap

01

Business Discovery

Understanding the business as it actually operates today — goals, constraints, and the decisions that matter most in the next 12 months.

02

Customer & Product Discovery

Structured research into what customers actually need, where the current product falls short, and where the real opportunity sits.

03

Product Analytics

Grounding the strategy in what the data actually shows — usage, retention, and the metrics that reveal what's working.

04

Market & Competitive Analysis

Sizing the opportunity and understanding the competitive landscape well enough to find a defensible position.

05

Product Strategy

Turning discovery and analysis into a clear, prioritized strategy the whole organization can align behind.

06

Product Roadmap

A sequenced, board-ready roadmap that translates strategy into decisions your team can execute.

07

Execution & Growth

Supporting the roadmap through delivery — prioritization, KPI review, and course-correction as real-world data comes in.

08

Knowledge Transfer

Making sure the thinking behind every decision lives inside your team, not just inside a deck.

Why It's Not Just a Framework

The framework focuses on useful decisions and deliverables. Each phase produces a real, specific deliverable — not a slide restating the obvious. The kind of rigor most large consulting firms charge millions for, applied with the precision and accountability of a boutique partner.

It's the operating system behind every Solante engagement, whether that's a focused Product Strategy Sprint, an embedded Fractional CPO retainer, or a full Enterprise Transformation program.
