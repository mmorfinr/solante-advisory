About

Built From the Inside of the Hardest Problems in Technology

Margarita Morfin — Founder

I started my career as an engineer — solving complex technical problems across Latin America for some of the world's largest telecommunications networks. Over fifteen years, that work evolved into executive product and program leadership at AT&T, T-Mobile, and global technology firms.

I've sat in the rooms where billion-dollar technology decisions get made. I've launched products with no playbook, transformed operations at a scale most organizations never reach, and led the kind of cross-functional programs that define whether a company wins or loses its next decade.

I founded Solante Advisory because the best strategic thinking shouldn't be locked inside large consulting firms. Founders and executives deserve direct access to the caliber of thinking that has historically only been available to the Fortune 50 — delivered with the speed, accountability, and personal investment of a boutique partner.
