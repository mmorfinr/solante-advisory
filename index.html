About

Built From the Inside of Complex Technology

Margarita Morfin — Founder & Principal

I started my career as an engineer solving complex telecommunications problems across Latin America and the United States, with experience spanning major global telecommunications vendors. Over the next 15+ years, that work evolved into product, technology, and program leadership across AT&T, T-Mobile, and global technology organizations.

I've led 0-to-1 product launches, large-scale platform modernization, connectivity products, AI-enabled initiatives, and complex cross-functional programs — from strategy through commercialization and scale.

I founded Solante Advisory to bring that combination of technical depth, product thinking, and business strategy directly to founders and organizations that need experienced leadership without the layers of a traditional consulting firm.

My academic foundation includes a degree in Telecommunications Engineering, an MBA, a Master's in Management, Strategy & Leadership, and a Master's in AI Software Engineering — complemented by professional certifications in Project Management, Agile, Cloud, and AI Product Leadership.
